About

Val Enfys is a DJ and audiovisual artist based in the UK, inspired by London’s warehouse and free party scene. Her techno sets blend driving grooves and high-energy transitions with emotional vocal intermissions. Drawing on her background as a software engineer, she is exploring live, 360° visual worlds that surround the dancefloor.

In 2026, Val was selected for the inaugural ADE Lab On Track, a three-day programme for early career DJs and producers, at the 30th edition of Amsterdam Dance Event. In London, she has organised and played her own forest party, played Fragment’s open decks, and played E1 in November 2026 alongside Alarico and Inox Traxx. She has also held a residency on London-based Sibling Radio, and has recorded mixes for international platforms including OMIDOX (Hamburg) and Over.The.Techno (Rostov-on-Don).

Val was drawn to techno through London’s club circuit and warehouse scene, where she met likeminded DJs and producers who brought her deeper into this world. She grew up playing bassoon, piano and guitar, giving her a strong sense of music theory to go with the practical experience of being on the dancefloor.

Val is currently studying Music Production and Software Engineering at pointblank Music School, working towards her own productions in Ableton Live. On the visual side, she is developing AV performances with tools like Videosync, TouchDesigner and Blender, as well as her own custom software.
